Maritime and cruise jobs, from your phone

CrewApply connects seafarers and maritime professionals with recruiters and shipping companies hiring for deck, engine, hospitality, catering and medical roles at sea.

What CrewApply does

CrewApply is a mobile job platform for the maritime and cruise industry. Candidates create a maritime profile, upload the certificates a role requires, and apply to listings directly from the app. Recruiters and shipping companies post the listings and make all hiring decisions.

We are a job-discovery and application platform. We do not employ candidates, and we do not guarantee placement, interviews or hiring outcomes.

For candidates

  • Search and filter live openings by department, rank, vessel type and location
  • Build a maritime CV — sea experience, rank, vessel history, certifications and availability
  • Store certificates securely: passport, CDC, STCW, medical, visa and other documents
  • Generate a formatted PDF résumé from your profile, in a choice of templates
  • Track every application and its status in one place
  • Get alerts when a job matching your rank and department is posted

Subscription plans

CrewApply is free to download and use. A free account can browse every listing and build a full profile. A paid subscription raises the number of applications you may submit each month and unlocks higher-tier listings. Prices shown here are indicative — the exact price, currency and any active launch offer are always displayed in the app before you pay.
