Delete Your CrewApply Account

Last updated 29 August 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your CrewApply account and the data associated with it. CrewApply is published by CrewApply. Deletion is free, and you do not need an active subscription to request it.

How to request deletion

Send us an email and we will delete your account. There are three steps:

  1. Email crewapplyjobs@gmail.com from the email address registered on your CrewApply account. Sending from the registered address is how we confirm the request is genuinely yours.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account. This routes your request straight to the deletion queue rather than general support.
  3. Send it. Nothing further is required from you. We acknowledge the request within 2 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days of that acknowledgement.

If we cannot match the sending address to an account, we will reply and ask you to send the request from the registered address. We will not delete an account on the instruction of an address we cannot verify, because doing so would let anyone delete someone else's account.

What is deleted

When your request is processed, the following are permanently removed and cannot be recovered:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number, date of birth, nationality and location
  • Your maritime CV: rank, department, sea experience, vessel history and availability
  • Every document you uploaded — passport, CDC, STCW certificates, medical certificate, visa and any other certificates
  • Your profile photograph
  • Résumés you generated in the app, and the résumé templates and settings saved to your account
  • Your saved jobs, job alerts and notification preferences
  • Device identifiers held for push notifications

What is kept, and for how long

A small amount of information is retained after deletion. We keep only what we are required to keep, and nothing that is retained is used to contact you or to build a profile of you.

What is kept Why, and for how long
Payment and subscription records — amount, date, transaction reference Required by tax and accounting law. Retained for as long as the applicable law requires, up to 8 years from the transaction date. Retained by our payment provider, Razorpay, under their own retention obligations.
Job applications already submitted to a recruiter Once you apply, the recruiter or shipping company holds a copy of that application as an independent controller. We remove our copy, but we cannot delete theirs. Contact the recruiter directly to request removal from their records.
Records relating to fraud, abuse or a breach of our Terms Kept where necessary to prevent the same activity recurring, and only for as long as that purpose requires — no longer than 3 years.
Server and security logs Rotated automatically and deleted within 90 days. Not linked back to your account once it is deleted.

Before you delete

  • Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore an account or its documents once the request has been processed. If you sign up again later, you start with an empty profile.
  • Download anything you want to keep first — in particular any résumé you generated, since we cannot recover it afterwards.
  • An active subscription is not refunded on deletion. Deleting your account ends access to a term you have already paid for. If you want a refund as well, request it separately and first — see our Cancellation and Refunds policy.

Deleting only some of your data

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove specific information. Most profile fields and every uploaded document can be edited or deleted directly in the app, on the Profile and Documents screens. If you want a particular category removed but wish to keep your account, email us and say what you want removed.

Questions

Email crewapplyjobs@gmail.com. How we handle your information generally is set out in our Privacy Policy, and our contact details are on the Contact page.
