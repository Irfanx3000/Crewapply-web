Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

This policy explains what information CrewApply collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It is the same policy shown inside the app.

1. Introduction

CrewApply ("we", "us", "our") operates the CrewApply mobile application and related services (the "Platform"), connecting seafarers and maritime professionals with job opportunities. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or otherwise using the Platform, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account details you provide: name, email, phone number, date of birth, gender, nationality, and location.
  • Maritime profile details: rank, department, sea experience, certifications, vessel history, and availability.
  • Documents you upload: resumes, passports, CDCs, medical certificates, STCW/other certificates, and visas — used strictly for job applications and eligibility checks.
  • Application activity: jobs viewed, saved, and applied to, and communications with recruiters through the Platform.
  • Payment information: subscription and consultancy payments are processed by our payment provider (Razorpay); we do not store your full card or bank details ourselves.
  • Device information: device type, operating system, app version, and a push-notification token, used to deliver job alerts and account notifications.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: operate and improve the Platform; match you with relevant job listings; process and track your applications; verify your eligibility for specific roles based on documents and subscription tier; process payments and manage your subscription; send you job alerts, application-status updates, and account notifications; provide customer support; and detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share your profile and submitted documents with recruiters and shipping companies you choose to apply to — this is the core purpose of the Platform. We also share limited information with service providers who help us operate the Platform (hosting, payment processing, email/SMS delivery, and push notifications), each bound to use it only to provide that service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access-controlled document storage — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and application data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 8).

7. Your Rights and Choices

You can review and update most of your profile information directly within the app. You may request a copy of your data, request correction of inaccurate data, or request deletion of your account by contacting us using the details in Section 10. You can also manage push-notification permissions from your device settings at any time.

8. Account Deletion

Deleting your account removes your profile from active use on the Platform. Some information may be retained where required for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes, or where it forms part of an already-submitted job application that a recruiter still needs to process. For the exact steps to request deletion, and a full list of what is deleted and what is kept, see Delete Your CrewApply Account.

9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is intended for individuals who meet the minimum working age applicable in their jurisdiction and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above, and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at crewapplyjobs@gmail.com.
