Support

For help with your account, a subscription, a payment or a document upload, email crewapplyjobs@gmail.com from the address registered on your account — it lets us find your account without asking you for details over email.

We aim to respond within 2 business days.

Helpful things to include

  • The email address on your CrewApply account
  • For a payment query, the payment reference shown in the app
  • For a technical problem, your device model and app version

Billing and refunds

Billing questions go to the same address. Before writing in, it may answer your question faster to read our Cancellation and Refunds policy, which sets out what is refundable and how to request one.

Privacy and data requests

To request a copy of your data, correct something inaccurate, or delete your account, email crewapplyjobs@gmail.com with the subject line Data request. What we hold and how long we keep it is set out in our Privacy Policy.

In the app

CrewApply also has a Help & Support option in the app menu, which sends your message with your account already attached — usually the quickest route if you are already signed in.
