Shipping and Delivery

Last updated [date you publish this]

CrewApply sells no physical goods. Nothing is packed, posted or couriered, and no delivery address is ever required. This page exists because payment providers require a delivery policy, and it describes how a purely digital service is delivered.

1. What is delivered

CrewApply sells access to features inside the CrewApply mobile application — a higher monthly job-application allowance, access to higher-tier job listings, and, where offered, consultancy sessions. All of it is delivered electronically within the app to the account that made the payment.

2. When it is delivered

Immediately on successful payment. Once our payment provider confirms the transaction, the subscription is activated on your account and the paid features become available straight away. There is no waiting period, dispatch step or delivery window.

A payment confirmed while the app is closed is still applied to your account; the features are active the next time you open it.

3. Where it is delivered

To the CrewApply account that was signed in when the purchase was made. You must be signed in to buy a subscription, which is what ties the purchase to your account. Access travels with the account, so signing in on a different device carries your subscription with you — no re-purchase, no transfer step.

4. Delivery charges

None. There are no shipping, handling or delivery fees of any kind. The price shown in the app before payment is the full amount charged.

5. Service availability

CrewApply is available to download and use internationally. Prices are shown in Indian rupees for users in India and in US dollars elsewhere; the applicable currency and amount are always displayed before you pay.

6. If access does not appear

In the rare case that a payment succeeds but the paid features do not activate, the subscription screen in the app reconciles the payment with our provider automatically the next time it is opened, and activates access if the payment did in fact go through.

If it still does not appear, email support@crewapply.com with the payment reference shown in the app. We will either activate your subscription or refund the payment — see our Cancellation and Refunds policy.

7. Contact

Questions about delivery of the service: support@crewapply.com. Full details are on our Contact page.
