Cancellation and Refunds

Last updated 29 August 2026

In short: subscriptions are prepaid fixed terms that do not auto-renew, so there is no recurring charge to cancel. A refund may be requested within 7 working days of payment where the paid features have not been substantially used, and we complete approved refunds within 7 working days of approval.

1. What you are buying

A CrewApply subscription is a prepaid, fixed-term licence to use paid features of the CrewApply app — a higher monthly application allowance and access to higher-tier job listings. It is a digital service. Nothing physical is dispatched.

2. Subscription term and renewal

Subscriptions are purchased for a fixed term: 30 days for a monthly plan and 365 days for a yearly plan. Access begins as soon as payment is confirmed and continues until the end of that term.

Subscriptions do not renew automatically. No payment is taken from you again unless you deliberately purchase another term. There is therefore no recurring charge to cancel.

3. Cancelling

Because nothing renews automatically, "cancelling" simply means choosing not to buy another term. Your existing subscription continues to work until the day it expires, and your account, profile and documents remain yours afterwards on the free tier.

If you want your account and data removed entirely, see Account Deletion in our Privacy Policy.

4. Refunds

If a payment was taken in error, charged twice, or did not result in your subscription being activated, contact us and we will investigate and refund in full where the fault is ours.

For other requests, a refund may be considered where all of the following apply:

  • the request is made within 7 working days of the payment;
  • the paid features have not been substantially used during that period — in particular, job applications submitted using the subscription's allowance;
  • the subscription was purchased directly through the CrewApply app.

Where a refund is approved, we refund the amount actually paid to the original payment method. Requests outside these conditions are assessed case by case; approval is at our discretion.

5. What is not refundable

  • A term that has already been substantially used — the allowance it granted has been consumed.
  • Dissatisfaction with hiring outcomes. CrewApply is a job-discovery platform: we do not employ candidates and cannot guarantee interviews, offers or placement. Recruiters and shipping companies make all hiring decisions.
  • Access lost because an account was suspended for a breach of our Terms and Conditions.

6. Upgrading mid-term

If you move to a higher plan while a subscription is still running, the price you are shown already accounts for the value remaining on your current plan. Nothing is lost by upgrading, and the change is not a cancellation — no refund arises from it.

7. Consultancy bookings

Where consultancy sessions are offered and paid for in the app, a booking may be cancelled and refunded in full if cancelled at least 24 hours before the scheduled start. Later cancellations and no-shows are not refundable, as the slot cannot be reallocated.

8. How to request a refund

Email crewapplyjobs@gmail.com from the address registered on your account, including the payment reference shown in the app. We acknowledge requests within 2 working days and complete approved refunds within 7 working days of approval.

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through our payment provider, Razorpay. Once we release a refund, the time it takes to appear on your statement is set by your bank or card issuer — typically 5 to 10 business days — and is outside our control.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: crewapplyjobs@gmail.com. Full details are on our Contact page.
